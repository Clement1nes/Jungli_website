## Design Context

### Users
Mix of spiritual/mystical youth, fashion-forward adults, and gift buyers. The spiritual/artisan angle is the core identity. Users are drawn to handcrafted, meaningful jewelry with stories behind each piece.

### Brand Personality
Mystical, raw, earthy — like stepping into an ancient forest temple. Organic, grounded, spiritual.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Dark, immersive environments with nature-inspired animated backgrounds
- Custom "Evil Green Plant" display font as the sole brand typeface
- Color palette: deep charcoal (#1A1A1D), warm cream (#fff5da), sage green (#8faf70), gold (#D4AF37), silver (#C0C0C0)
- Hand-drawn underline motifs on interactive elements
- Products float weightlessly in their environments
- Rock/stone texture buttons (SVG filtered)
- No rounded corners, no glassmorphism — raw edges preferred

### Design Principles
1. **Earthy over polished** — imperfections are intentional, not bugs
2. **The jewelry is the hero** — backgrounds serve the product, never compete
3. **Storytelling drives everything** — each ring has lore, the UI should feel like a journey
4. **Consistent craft** — spacing, typography hierarchy, and transitions should feel deliberate
5. **Dark mode is the only mode** — this is a night gallery, not a daytime shop
